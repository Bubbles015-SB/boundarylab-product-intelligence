THE ORIGINAL INSIGHT

Binary adoption is a poor question

R/11

A customer can accept a service in one situation and reject the same service in another.

Salonee's application materials describe 11 qualitative interviews and a shift from a yes/no adoption framing towards individual conditions, including product category, home versus work, and concerns over courier treatment.

WORKSPACE 04 / DECISION FRONTIER

Trade-offs are the strategy.

Evaluate up to 126 combinations of courier safeguards, delivery fees and promised ETAs. Find options that balance modelled acceptance with net contribution while enforcing non-negotiable protections.

The Pareto frontier marks scenarios where no other tested scenario achieves both higher interest and higher contribution. It is scenario analysis, not a causal or market-validated optimisation result.

DECISION DISCIPLINE

What the model cannot decide

  • Whether customers in the real world have these distributions or thresholds.
  • What actual courier pay protections and delivery costs must be.
  • Whether a financially attractive offer complies with employment laws.
  • Whether observed conversions improve because of the tested change.

TECHNICAL DESIGN

Reproducible & testable

  • 1,200 generated fictional profiles; fixed random seed.
  • Deterministic person-level rule evaluation and propensity model.
  • Explicit financial assumptions and capacity constraints.
  • Discrete constrained optimisation with Pareto filtering.
  • Prespecified two-proportion test planning.
  • Automated Node.js tests and Python dataset integrity checks.
