# Interview walkthrough — what Salonee should know before including BoundaryLab

## Five-minute demonstration

1. **Context (45 sec):** Dissertation's reported qualitative outcome was conditional rather than binary adoption. No empirical proportions are available in the prototype.
2. **Evidence (45 sec):** Show the evidence ledger and one row linking a reported claim to a clearly *untested* product hypothesis.
3. **Eligibility (60 sec):** Change courier policy to the baseline tier. Explain hard vetoes and the importance of not treating worker safeguards as simply another optimisation weight.
4. **Economics (60 sec):** Change the delivery fee; explain how demand, capacity-limited orders and modelled contribution differ.
5. **Experimentation (60 sec):** Show the sample-size calculator, randomisation and predeclared safety guardrails.
6. **Limitations (30 sec):** Explicitly state that respondents are synthetic, preferences uncalibrated and causality unproven.

## Honest portfolio description (adapt only after participation)

> **BoundaryLab — Conditional Adoption Research-to-Decision Prototype.** Research-inspired interactive case study exploring how product category, context and worker-treatment conditions can shape rapid-delivery eligibility. Features a deterministic synthetic-persona simulator, sensitivity and unit-economics scenarios, rule-level traceability, constrained decision frontiers and a planned A/B-test framework. All scenario outputs are illustrative, not real customer findings.

## Difficult interview questions

**Did your dissertation demonstrate that 12% of users would adopt?** No. The 12% displayed in a scenario comes from invented model assumptions. The qualitative work did not supply a statistically generalisable population estimate.

**Are the worker protection scores real?** No. They are an explicitly fictional modelling device. Live products would use concrete independently checked protections.

**What is the strongest PM skill shown?** Translating ambiguous qualitative findings into testable requirements, guardrails, instrumentation and falsifiable hypotheses.

**What would you change with real data?** Ground threshold distributions in properly consented observation, calibrate the propensity model and test the intervention with controlled assignment.

**Did you build this alone?** The initial implementation was AI-generated. Explain accurately which parts were reviewed, tested or extended personally.

**Can you publish the actual interviews?** Not without consent and compliance with the study's ethics approval. This repository deliberately excludes them.
